# Character Research

## Purpose

Research the developer character before designing it. The character should feel like a minimal
vector companion, not an anime character and not a realistic portrait.

## Character Direction

Core idea:

- Minimalist female developer.
- Hoodie.
- Backpack.
- Laptop.
- Smart watch.
- Small glowing blue bracelet.
- Long hair with subtle motion.

## References To Collect

Target: 20 references.

Track each reference with:

- Source or link.
- What works.
- What to avoid.
- Detail to reuse in an original way.

## Rules

- Keep the character simple enough to animate.
- Avoid making the character distract from content.
- Make the silhouette readable at small sizes.
- Keep details meaningful and limited.
