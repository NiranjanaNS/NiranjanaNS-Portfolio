# Inspiration Research

This document tracks inspiration without copying. The goal is to study why strong product and
portfolio experiences work, then translate those lessons into original decisions.

## Research Method

For each reference, capture:

- What I like.
- What I do not like.
- Ideas to use.
- Ideas to avoid.

## Early Direction

Useful ideas:

- Clear hero message before visual complexity.
- Smooth scroll-based storytelling.
- Project pages that explain decisions, not only outcomes.
- Calm dark background with strong contrast.
- Reusable components that make the product feel consistent.
- Technical documentation as part of the project identity.

Ideas to avoid:

- Overloaded animation that slows down navigation.
- Mystery navigation.
- Tiny text and inaccessible contrast.
- Portfolio sections that look impressive but say very little.
- Skill percentages that do not communicate real ability.
