# Animation Research

## Purpose

Study animation choices before implementing them. The goal is to understand why motion improves
the experience, not just how to recreate effects.

## Research Questions

- What does the animation help the user understand?
- Does it guide attention or create confusion?
- Does it make the interface feel faster or slower?
- Can the content still work without the animation?
- Is the effect worth the performance cost?

## Motion Categories To Study

### Entrance

Observe:

- How sections appear.
- Whether content enters in groups or one item at a time.
- How long the animation lasts.

## Ideas To Use

- Subtle staggered reveals.
- Scroll-linked parallax for environment layers.
- Hover feedback on interactive crystals and project rooms.
- Reduced-motion fallback for all major scene movement.

## Ideas To Avoid

- Motion that blocks reading.
- Long loading animations.
- Scroll effects that trap the user.
- Hover-only content on mobile.
- Heavy effects before the core content is visible.
